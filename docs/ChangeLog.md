# Release 0.1.0

The first release: a quiz party game for a Mac and phones, on the Mac App Store and the App Store, and built from
source.

## New

- **The party.** The Mac runs the party and shows the big screen, on a TV or projector. Players on the same Wi-Fi join
  by scanning its QR code, or by going to its address and typing the game code, and answer multiple-choice questions,
  scoring more the faster they answer. A party plays several quizzes, with the reveal and the standings after each
  question, and leaderboards for each quiz and for the whole party.
- **Hosting.** The host runs the party from the Mac, or from an iPhone paired with it: moving on, pausing, removing a
  player or ending the party. A party can also move on by itself.
- **The iOS app,** for players, which finds parties on the Wi-Fi by itself, or scans the join code, and for the host.
  Players without it join in the browser.
- **Writing quizzes.** Quizzes are written and edited on the Mac, with a graph of the points a correct answer scores as
  time passes, and each question's image chosen from the quiz's folder.
- **Quizzes written with Claude.** Write a Quiz with Claude reads Wikipedia's articles on a topic and has Claude draft
  the questions, with your own Claude API key, kept in the Keychain, and the model you choose, crediting the articles
  and the freely licensed images it uses. The draft opens in the editor to review. Built from source, it can also use
  Claude Code, on your own Claude subscription.
- **Libraries.** Quizzes are shared in libraries, folders of quizzes on the web or on disk; the Quizzes page lists
  Bruce's, or any other, and downloads quizzes from it. A quizzes folder chosen on the Mac is remembered, so it can be
  read again the next time the app runs.
